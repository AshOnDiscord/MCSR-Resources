Technically speaking, you can speedrun Minecraft in any versions and in plain vanilla. However for the purpose of this guide, we will be focusing on 1.16.1 RSG (and FSG) and with the currently whitelisted mod-list. 1.16.1 is the current meta version for speedrunning because of the existence of bastions while still lacking reduced piglin trade rates or piglin brutes like later versions. We will also be sticking to things that are speedrun.com (referenced as SRC throughout the guide) verifiable.

# Prism Launcher
Prism Launcher, the modern fork of MultiMC, is not just the go to for most Minecraft in general but also for MCSR. Install Prism according the instructions on [prismlauncher.org/download](https://prismlauncher.org/download/?from=button), and follow the setup instructions

## Creating our Instance
After install prism launcher, press the `Add Instance` button in the upper left corner. In the window that opens, set your version to `1.16.1` and select `Fabric` as your mod loader. Set your fabric version to the suggested version (the version with a star next to it).

After you have you instance created, right click, and press edit in the context menu. On the sidebar, select `Settings`, you should be inside the `Java` tab by default, if not switch over. Then check `Java installation` and `Skip Java compatibility checks`. ### Installing Mods
While mods aren't necessary by any mean, they are encouraged as it is hardly any setup and you gain tons of quality of life features and performance boost. Open [this page](https://mods.tildejustin.dev/?version=1.16.1&run=rsg&os=linux) in your browser. On the page, scroll down and check `Accessibility Mods` under `Additional Options`, this page consists of all allowed speedrunning mods for SRC. In order to download the mods, just click on each mod in the list and press download.

Once you have all the mods downloaded, switch back to prism launcher, switch from `Settings` to `Mods` in your left sidebar. Now click `Add file` on the right sidebar and select all the mods you just downloaded in the file explorer.
### Mod Usage
Yay! You have your basic instance setup for speedrunning, you might notice a button with golden boots to the left of your singleplayer, we'll get to that later. For now let's focus on getting your minecraft settings correct.
#### Standard Settings
You might notice whenever you open a world, it seems like none of your minecraft settings have changed. That's for a good reason. Standard Settings, one of the mods that we downloaded, resets your settings on every world to a preconfigured default. Right now though its not particularly useful, so how do we configure the default? In your options menu, there is a button with a book and quill. Clicking it will bring you to the MCSR version of Mod Menu. Find standard settings and double click it.

You're now in the config menu of standard settings, here you can set all your settings up. If you have certain options you don't want to reset like your volume or settings that aren't already reset that you would like, on the right, there is a toggle button for every option.

Near the bottom there will be a setting called `Pie Directory` under `F3 Settings...`. You don't have to worry about what this does, just set it for now to `root.gameRenderer.level.entities` (just copy and paste). When you are all done, just click done to exit the Standard Settings config menu
